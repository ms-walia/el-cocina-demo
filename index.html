Somewhereyou losean evening

We began with a simple disagreement: that dining out in Amritsar should feel like an occasion, not an errand. So we built a room with low light and long tables, hired a kitchen that argues about salt, and let the menu wander wherever the produce leads. Come for two hours. Stay for four.
